Web games by Maxim Ponomarev

Browser games I designed and built with AI tools (Claude Code for code, generated art and sound). Made for Yandex Games and CrazyGames. Everything runs in the browser, no install.

Not a Sound first-person horror · Three.js

A stalker hunts you by sound, not by sight: he walks to where he heard the noise. Freeze with your light off and you disappear for him. Seven story nights, hiding spots, notes to collect.

WASD move · Shift sneak · F flashlight · C hide

Ghostfire voxel FPS · Three.js

1v1 duel against a ghost: a recording of a real player's movement that aims at you live. Beat it and your own ghost is recorded, ready to send to a friend by link. Six weapons, shop, skins and a built-in map editor.

WASD move · mouse aim and shoot · R reload

Gaff: Ice Duel 1v1 arcade duel

Short duels on ice against five rivals with rising difficulty, first to three wins. Practice mode, daily challenge, cosmetics.
